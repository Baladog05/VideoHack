How Haystack works

An agent that turns raw street and dashcam video into a labelled dataset of the rare moments that trip up self-driving cars.

1IndexVideo on VAST storage runs through the VSS pipeline: YOLO detections, NVIDIA Cosmos-Reason2 scene descriptions, Cosmos-Embed1 vectors in VastDB.
2Search28 edge-case searches across 7 categories, or anything you type into Ask.
3JudgeAn LLM on W&B Inference reads the clip plus the clips before and after, with detector motion, and decides if it's a real edge case.
4Fill gapsThe gap-filling agent finds the weakest category, re-ingests the most promising videos through VSS with a prompt focused on it, searches again and adds what it finds. Humans confirm or reject in one key press.
5ExportClips, per-frame boxes, labels and a dataset card, versioned as a W&B artifact.

What the value score means

How useful a clip is as training data, out of 10.

SignalFromWeight
Edge-case scoreAI judge: how rare and dangerous55%
AV difficultyAI judge: how hard for a self-driving car20%
MotionYOLO boxes: road users growing fast in the car's path15%
Category rarityFewer examples in the dataset = more valuable10%

A clip counts as a needle when the judge says it's an edge case, it isn't routine, and its edge-case score is 5 or more.
