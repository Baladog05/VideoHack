How it works

Finding the needles in thousands of hours of driving video

Self-driving systems fail on rare moments: a pedestrian stepping out from behind a truck, a cyclist running a red, a vehicle reversing into traffic. Those moments are a tiny fraction of real footage. Haystack is an agent that finds them, explains them and packages them as labelled training data.

The pipeline

1Index

Footage stored on VAST is processed by the VSS pipeline: YOLO11 object detection, NVIDIA Cosmos-Reason2 scene descriptions, and Cosmos-Embed1 vectors stored in VastDB.

2Search

Haystack runs 28 targeted semantic searches across 7 edge-case categories, plus any question you type, to pull a shortlist of candidate clips.

3Judge

An LLM on W&B Inference reviews each candidate with the clips before and after it and the detector's motion signal, then decides whether it is a genuine edge case and why.

4Score

Every edge case gets a value out of 10, combining how rare and dangerous it is, how hard it is for an autonomous stack, motion in the car's path, and category rarity.

5Review

A person confirms or rejects each clip with one key press. Those verdicts measure the judge's accuracy and decide what goes into the dataset.

6Export

Clips, per-frame boxes, COCO annotations, a train/val split and a dataset card are packaged into a zip and versioned as a W&B artifact.

Using the site

Search

Ask for a scenario in plain English, for example “cyclist running a red light”. Haystack searches the index, judges the matches and shows which ones are real edge cases. Use ↑ ↓ to step through results.

 Live agent

Press Go live and the agent works through unchecked footage camera by camera, like a safety monitor. Routine scenes are skipped; edge cases are added to the library and announced in the corner. It runs until you press Pause.

 Needles

Every edge case found so far, filterable by category, with the video, object boxes and the judge's explanation. Review with Y (correct) and N (wrong), and move with J K.

 Dataset

Coverage by category and the export. Choose a minimum value, a category, whether to include video, and whether to keep only human-confirmed clips. Clips a person rejected are always left out.
